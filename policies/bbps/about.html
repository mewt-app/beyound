About Us

Last updated: 1 April 2026

Welcome to Beyound BBPS Payments

BeyoundExtra Technologies Pvt Ltd is a pioneering payments and financial technology company revolutionising the way bill payments are made in India. We focus on leveraging the Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) operated by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) to provide a seamless, secure, and rewarding credit card payment experience for all your recurring bills.

We offer innovative solutions that enable you to pay electricity, water, gas, DTH, mobile postpaid, broadband, insurance premiums, loan EMIs, and other BBPS-eligible bills using your credit card.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple, to simplify and enhance the bill payment experience for every Indian household. We believe that paying bills should be convenient, secure, and efficient. By offering a platform that allows you to pay all your bills using a single credit card, we are breaking down barriers and empowering you to manage your monthly obligations in a hassle-free manner while maximising your credit card rewards.

What We Do

At Beyound BBPS Payments, we offer cutting-edge payment solutions integrated directly with the BBPS infrastructure. Our platform enables:

  • One-stop bill payments – Pay electricity, water, gas, DTH, mobile, broadband, insurance, loan EMIs, and more from a single app.

  • Instant bill fetch – View your bill amount and due date in real time using your consumer number.

  • Credit card payments – Use any Visa, MasterCard, or RuPay credit card to pay your bills.

  • Rewards on payment – Earn cashback and Beyound Rewards on every eligible bill transaction.

  • Instant confirmation – Receive a digital receipt and payment confirmation immediately after successful transaction.

We act as a BBPS-enabled technology facilitator, ensuring that your credit card payment reaches the respective biller through the secure NPCI network with complete transparency and reliability.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred BBPS payment partner for households across India. We envision a world where paying bills is no longer a chore, just a few clicks on your phone screen, with real-time confirmation, no hidden charges, and rewards on every payment.

Why Choose Beyound for BBPS Payments?

  • Unified Platform – Pay all your bills, utilities, telecom, insurance, loans and more from a single app without switching between multiple websites or apps.

  • Security First – We prioritise the safety of your data. Our platform uses bank-grade encryption, complies with RBI and NPCI guidelines, and integrates directly with BBPS for secure transaction routing.

  • Convenience – By allowing credit card payments for all your bills, you can manage your monthly cash flow better and enjoy up to 50 days of interest-free credit.

  • Rewards on Payment – Earn cashback and Beyound Rewards on every eligible bill payment, turning essential monthly expenses into rewarding opportunities.

  • Low & Transparent Fees – We charge a modest fee for BBPS transactions, disclosed clearly before every payment and no hidden charges.
