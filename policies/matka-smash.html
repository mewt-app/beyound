4. Campaign Mechanics

4.1. How to Play

  • a. The User is presented with a game screen displaying a series of items moving across the screen.
  • b. The User must tap or click on the following items to earn Rally Cash:
    • Matka (Clay Pot): A maximum of 1 Rally Cash per Matka hit.
    • Gold Item: A maximum of 2 Rally Cash per Gold item hit.
  • c. The User must avoid tapping or clicking on the following items:
    • Firecracker: Hitting a Firecracker results in the loss of one (1) Life.
    • Stone: Hitting a Stone results in the loss of one (1) Life.
  • d. If the User misses a Matka or a Stone (i.e., the item passes without being tapped), the User loses one (1) Life.

4.2. Lives

  • a. Each Game starts with three (3) Lives.
  • b. The Game ends when the User loses all three (3) Lives.
  • c. If the User loses all Lives, the Game ends and the User cannot continue that session.

4.3. Game Duration

Each Game lasts until the User loses all Lives or until the game timer expires (if applicable). Rally reserves the right to set a maximum duration for each Game.

4.4. Scoring

  • a. Rally Cash earned during a Game may be calculated based on the number of Matkas and Gold items successfully hit during that Game.
  • b. There is no upper limit on the amount of Rally Cash that can be earned in a single Game.
  • c. The final grant of Rally Cash at the end of each Game is at the sole discretion of Rally. Rally's decision shall be final and binding.
