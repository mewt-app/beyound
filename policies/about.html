About Beyound

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Beyound is a payments technology platform built and operated by BeyoundExtra Tech Private Limited, a company incorporated in India. We exist to make moving money as straightforward as the rest of your day — secure, modern, and built around how you actually pay.

What we do

Beyound runs two consumer services today:

  • Beyound Wallet — a prepaid digital wallet that lets you load funds via any supported payment mode (credit card, debit card, net banking, UPI) and transfer them onward to any bank account beneficiary. Available at wallet.beyound.in.
  • Beyound Bills (BBPS) — credit-card bill payments routed through the Bharat Bill Payment System for electricity, water, gas, DTH, broadband, postpaid mobile, insurance, education and more. Available at bills.beyound.in.

Both services run on the same compliance, security and grievance infrastructure operated by BeyoundExtra Tech Private Limited.

What we believe

  • Payments should be boring. The best transaction is one you don't have to think about. We obsess over the steps you don't see.
  • Security is the product. Beyound is built around KYC, fraud controls, and the network rules of RBI, NPCI and BBPCU — not bolted on after the fact.
  • Money should be portable. Credit-card rails, bank rails and bill-payment rails should all just work, without you needing to know which one you're on.

How we are regulated

Beyound is a technology and payment-facilitation platform. We do not provide banking, lending, deposit-taking, credit underwriting or financial-advisory services. Wallet load and bill-payment transactions are settled through authorised banking and payment-system partners. We operate strictly within the boundaries set by RBI, NPCI, the Bharat Bill Payment System and applicable Indian law.
