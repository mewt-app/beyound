ABOUT US

Welcome to Beyound Esolutions Private Limited, a pioneering payments and financial technology company revolutionizing the way transactions are made. At BeyoundExtra Technologies, we focus on bridging the gap between traditional payment systems and modern-day needs, offering innovative solutions that enable seamless and secure credit card transactions for education fees.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple – to simplify and enhance the financial experience for both businesses and individuals. We believe that financial transactions should be convenient, secure, and efficient. By offering a platform that allows tenants and students to make payments using their credit cards, we’re breaking down barriers and empowering people to manage their financial obligations in a hassle-free manner.

What We Do

At BeyoundExtra Technologies, we offer cutting-edge payment solutions designed specifically for education fee payments. Our platform enables students and parents to make seamless payments for tuition fees and other educational expenses using credit cards.

Our Vision

To become a leader in the payments and financial technology sector by continuously developing innovative solutions that meet the evolving needs of our customers. We envision a world where financial transactions, especially for recurring payments like education fees, are as simple as a few clicks on your phone screen.

  • Seamless Integration: Our technology integrates effortlessly with payment systems, allowing home-owners, businesses and institutions to accept credit card payments without disruption.

  • Convenience: By allowing credit card payments for regular expenses like tuition, we make managing your finances easier, faster, and more flexible.
  • Customer Support: Our dedicated support team is always ready to assist, ensuring you have a smooth experience with our services.
