HOW THE CONTEST WORKS

Prediction Window:
For each match, the Contest will open for predictions at 12:00:01 AM (IST) on the day of the match and will close 5 minutes before the match begins. No predictions will be accepted after the deadline.

Making a Prediction:
Eligible users can log in to the Beyound app, navigate to the Contest section, and select the team they believe will win the scheduled match. Each user is limited to one (1) prediction per match.

Correct Prediction Reward:
If the user correctly predicts the winning team, they will receive Super Cash equal to the number of runs scored by the winning team in that match (e.g., if the winning team scores 185 runs, the user receives 185 Super Cash points). Rewards are calculated based on the official scorecard published by the league.

Incorrect Prediction:
If the user predicts incorrectly, no Super Cash is awarded. No user cash, balance, or other value is deducted.

Crediting Rewards: Super Cash will be credited to the user’s Beyound wallet within one (1) hour of the official match result being declared. In case of a technical delay, Beyound reserves the right to credit within a reasonable time.

LEADERBOARD

How the Leaderboard Works: A cumulative leaderboard tracks each eligible user’s total Super Cash earned across all matches in the Contest period. Users are ranked in descending order of total Super Cash earned.

Viewing Your Rank: Users can view the current top rankings as well as their own rank at any time within the Contest section of the Beyound app.

Tiebreaker: In the event of a tie in total Super Cash earned, the user with the higher number of correct predictions will be ranked higher. If both total Super Cash and number of correct predictions are equal, the user who made their first correct prediction earlier (by timestamp) will be ranked higher.
