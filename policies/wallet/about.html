About Us

Last updated: 1 April 2026

Welcome to Beyound Wallet

BeyoundExtra Technologies Pvt Ltd is a pioneering payments and financial technology company revolutionising the way individuals and businesses move money. We focus exclusively on providing a secure, credit-card-funded digital wallet that enables you to load money from your credit card and transfer it instantly to any bank account, without the need for cash withdrawal or complex processes.

Our wallet solution is designed for those who want to maximise their credit card rewards, manage cash flow, and make bank transfers conveniently from a single, regulated platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple, to empower individuals with a flexible, secure, and rewarding way to transfer money using their credit cards. We believe that moving funds to any bank account should be as easy as a few taps on your phone. By offering a wallet that is funded directly from your credit card, we break down barriers, help you earn rewards on every load, and give you complete control over your payments while adhering to the highest regulatory standards.

What We Do

At Beyound Wallet, we offer a cutting-edge digital prepaid payment instrument (PPI) that allows you to:

  • Load money from any Visa, MasterCard, or RuPay credit card into your Beyound Wallet.

  • Transfer funds instantly to any bank account in India (beneficiary of your choice).

  • Complete Video KYC – a secure, live verification process that activates your wallet in minutes.

  • Track every transaction with real-time balance updates and digital receipts.

  • Earn rewards on every eligible load and transfer, turning essential money movements into rewarding opportunities.

Important Note: Funds in your Beyound Wallet cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another wallet. They are designed exclusively for bank transfers to legitimate beneficiaries.

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted credit-card-funded wallet for seamless bank transfers. We envision a world where anyone can use their credit card to pay rent, send money to family, settle vendor invoices, or make any other legitimate bank transfer without the hassle of cash handling, hidden fees, or security concerns.

Why Choose Beyound Wallet?

  • Credit Card Rewards – Load money using your credit card and earn reward points, cashback, and interest-free credit periods (up to 50 days) on every transaction.

  • Instant Bank Transfers – Send money to any bank account in India with just a few clicks. Transfers are processed quickly and securely.

  • Security First – We prioritise your safety. Our platform uses bank-grade encryption, and we conduct mandatory Video KYC to prevent fraud and ensure compliance with RBI’s PPI guidelines.

  • No Cash Withdrawal – Your wallet is designed for responsible transfers. Funds cannot be converted to cash, reducing the risk of misuse.

  • Low & Transparent Fees – We charge a modest fee for wallet loads and transfers, disclosed clearly before every transaction ensuring no hidden surprises.

  • Video KYC – Complete your identity verification from the comfort of your home via a live video call. Fast, secure, and paperless.

  • Dedicated Customer Support – Our support team is available 24×7 to assist you with any wallet-related queries or issues.

Join Us in Shaping the Future of Credit-Card-Funded Transfers

At Beyound Wallet, we are not just a payment service provider, we are your partner in smarter money movement. Whether you need to pay rent, send money to a vendor, support a family member, or manage business expenses, our wallet gives you the power to use your credit card for bank transfers efficiently, securely, and rewardingly.
