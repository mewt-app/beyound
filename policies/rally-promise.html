Rally Promise — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 05 August 2026

Introduction

Rally is committed to providing a seamless and reliable payment experience to its users. As part of this commitment, Rally introduces the Rally Promise, a goodwill initiative under which users may receive a refund in the form of RallyCash if a qualifying transaction initiated on the Platform is delayed beyond the expected settlement timeframe.

These Terms and Conditions ("T&Cs") govern the Rally Promise campaign. By using the Platform and participating in the Rally Promise, you agree to be bound by these T&Cs.

Definitions

"Rally Promise" means the campaign under which Rally offers RallyCash refunds for delayed settlement of qualifying transactions, as set out in these T&Cs.

"Qualifying Transaction" means a payment transaction initiated by a user on the Rally Platform for the purpose of paying utility bills, credit card bills, or other eligible payments as determined by Rally from time to time.

"Settlement" means the successful transfer of funds from the user's credit card (or other payment instrument) to the intended beneficiary's account, as confirmed by Rally's banking partner.

"Delay" means the period commencing from the time a Qualifying Transaction is successfully initiated by the user on the Platform (i.e., the transaction is authorised and confirmed) and ending at the time the Settlement is confirmed by Rally's banking partner.

"RallyCash" means the reward currency issued by Rally on its Platform, which can be redeemed against future transactions as per the applicable reward program.

"Transaction Value" means the total amount of the Qualifying Transaction, inclusive of all applicable charges, fees, and taxes paid by the user.

Eligibility

  1. The Rally Promise is available to all registered users of the Rally Platform who initiate a Qualifying Transaction.
  2. The Rally Promise applies only to Qualifying Transactions that are successfully completed (i.e., the transaction is authorised and funds are debited from the user's credit card). Transactions that fail, are declined, or are cancelled by the user or by the banking partner are not eligible.
  3. The Rally Promise applies only to delays in Settlement that occur after the successful initiation of the Qualifying Transaction. Any delay arising from the user's actions (e.g., incorrect beneficiary details, insufficient credit limit, or failure to complete authentication) is not covered.

Rally reserves the right to exclude certain categories of transactions from the Rally Promise, including but not limited to:

  • Transactions involving high-risk or prohibited merchant categories;
  • Transactions that are identified as fraudulent, suspicious, or in violation of Rally's Terms of Service;
  • Transactions that are self-funded, circular, or artificially inflated.

Refund Structure

Subject to these T&Cs, a user may be eligible to receive a refund in RallyCash based on the duration of the Delay, calculated as follows:

Delay Duration Refund as Percentage of Transaction Value
30 minutes to less than 2 hours 10%
2 hours to less than 6 hours 30%
6 hours or more 100%
  1. The refund shall be calculated on the Transaction Value (excluding any convenience fees, platform fees, or taxes, unless otherwise determined by Rally).
  2. The refund shall be credited to the user's RallyCash wallet within a reasonable time after the Settlement is confirmed and the Delay is verified.
  3. The refund is provided as a goodwill gesture and is not an admission of liability or fault on the part of Rally. The Rally Promise is not a guarantee of Settlement within any specific timeframe.

Calculation and Verification of Delay

  1. The Delay shall be calculated based on the timestamp recorded by Rally's systems at the time the Qualifying Transaction is successfully initiated, and the timestamp recorded by Rally's systems at the time the Settlement is confirmed by Rally's banking partner.
  2. The timestamps recorded by Rally's systems shall be final and conclusive for the purpose of determining eligibility and the applicable refund tier.
  3. Rally may rely on information provided by its banking partners, payment gateways, and card networks to verify Settlement times. Any discrepancy in such third-party data shall be resolved by Rally in its sole discretion.
  4. Rally reserves the right to verify each claim for refund under the Rally Promise and may request additional information from the user to process the claim.

RallyCash Refund — Validity and Usage

  1. The RallyCash refunded under the Rally Promise shall be credited to the user's RallyCash wallet and shall be valid for a period of seventy-two (72) hours from the time of credit ("Validity Period").
  2. Any RallyCash not utilised within the Validity Period shall automatically expire and be forfeited, without any compensation or extension.
  3. RallyCash refunded under the Rally Promise may be used by the user to offset the transaction value of future Qualifying Transactions on the Platform.

RallyCash refunded under the Rally Promise:

  • Cannot be withdrawn or redeemed for cash;
  • Cannot be transferred to another user or account;
  • Cannot be combined with other promotional offers, unless expressly permitted by Rally;
  • Is subject to the general RallyCash terms and conditions as set out in the Platform's reward program.

Rally reserves the right to deduct or reverse any RallyCash credited under the Rally Promise if:

  • The Qualifying Transaction is subsequently reversed, charged back, or refunded;
  • The user is found to have violated any of these T&Cs or the Platform's Terms of Service;
  • The refund was issued in error or based on incorrect information.

Exclusions and Limitations

The Rally Promise shall not apply in the following circumstances:

  • Delays caused by the user, including incorrect beneficiary details, insufficient credit limit, or failure to complete authentication;
  • Delays caused by the beneficiary bank, including technical failures, processing delays, or account verification issues;
  • Delays caused by card networks (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) or card-issuing banks;
  • Delays arising from force majeure events, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, natural disasters, strikes, government orders, or regulatory actions;
  • Delays arising from scheduled maintenance, upgrades, or downtime of the Platform (with prior notice to users, where possible);
  • Delays arising from fraud, suspected fraud, or anti-money laundering holds imposed by Rally or its banking partners;
  • Delays for transactions that are not Qualifying Transactions.
  1. Rally makes no representation or warranty that Settlement will occur within any specific timeframe, and the Rally Promise is not a guarantee of Settlement time.
  2. Rally's liability under the Rally Promise is limited to the refund of RallyCash as set out in these T&Cs, and Rally shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages arising from any Delay.

In such cases, Rally may, at its sole discretion:
