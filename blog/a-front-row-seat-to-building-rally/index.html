A Front-Row Seat to
Building Rally

Notes from inside the Growth & Acquisition team — the campaigns that worked, the ones that didn't, and why never quite knowing what will work is the best part of the job.

By Shreekta Growth & Acquisition, BeyoundExtra Tech 21 August 2026 3 min read

People often ask me what it's like working in Growth.

Honestly?
It's a lot of fun.

Not because everything works.
Actually, quite the opposite.

A big part of Growth is trying things that you think will work, watching them not work, figuring out why, and then trying again.

And that's exactly what makes building Rally so exciting.

Today, Rally is among the top 5 credit card bill payment applications in India.

When you see that as a number, it sounds impressive.

But when you're sitting inside the Growth & Acquisition team, you see everything that went into getting there.

The campaigns that worked.
The campaigns that didn't.
The users who dropped off at a step we thought was perfectly fine.
The experiment where changing one small thing moved the numbers.
The idea we were convinced would be brilliant that users simply didn't care about.

And then, back to the drawing board.

That's probably my favourite thing about Growth.

You never really know what will work.

You have data, of course. You have funnels, cohorts, dashboards and a lot of spreadsheets.

But eventually, you have to understand people.

Why did someone choose to make their payment with us?
Why didn't someone come back?
Why did someone click on one message but completely ignore another?
Why would someone trust Rally with their money?

Those questions are far more interesting than just looking at a conversion percentage.

And because we're building a payments product, there's an added layer to it.

We're not selling something people buy once and forget about.

We're asking them to trust us with their money.

So every little thing matters.

The way we communicate.
The way the product feels.
How quickly a payment goes through.
How quickly it settles.
How easy it is to understand what just happened.

And when all of those things come together and you see someone make their first payment, then their second, and eventually make Rally part of their regular routine — that's a pretty great feeling.

The Growth team gets to sit right in the middle of all of this.

You work with Product.
You work with Design.
You work with Data.
You work with Engineering.
You work with Marketing.

And somewhere between all of that, you're constantly asking:

"What can we do better?"

Sometimes the answer is a new campaign.
Sometimes it's a product change.
Sometimes it's just changing a few words.

And sometimes, it's accepting that your idea wasn't as good as you thought it was.

That's the fun of building something.

You're never completely sure what's around the corner.

Being among the top 5 is a milestone we're proud of.

But being on the inside, I don't think about it as "We've made it."

I think about it as:

We're building something that people are choosing. Now, how do we make them choose us even more?

And that's what makes this journey exciting.

We're still experimenting.
Still learning.
Still getting things wrong.
Still finding things that work.
And still building.

One idea, one experiment and one user at a time.

That's what Growth at Rally feels like to me.

A little chaotic.
A little obsessive.
And honestly, a lot of fun.

S
Shreekta Growth & Acquisition, BeyoundExtra Tech Private Limited.
