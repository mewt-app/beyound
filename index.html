About

Payments should be boring.

Beyound is operated by BeyoundExtra Tech Private Limited, an independent payments technology company incorporated in India. We do one thing: make moving money between credit cards, wallets, banks and billers feel like a single, unbroken motion.

The best transaction is one you don't have to think about — and the plumbing behind it is exactly what we obsess over. Read the full About Us.

Bangalore, India · Founded 2025

  • Security is the product. Built around KYC, fraud controls, and the network rules of RBI, NPCI and BBPCU — not bolted on after the fact.
  • Money should be accessible. Credit-card rails, bank rails and bill-payment rails should all just work, without you needing to know which one you're on.
  • We are a facilitator. Beyound does not provide banking, lending or deposit-taking services. Transactions are settled through authorised banking and payment-system partners.
  • Grievance, in writing. Every escalation route, timeline and contact is published in our Grievance Redressal Policy.
