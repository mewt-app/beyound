FAQ & Definitions

Common questions, defined terms.

Most of the words that show up across the Beyound Wallet and Beyound Bills policies. The questions at the bottom answer what users ask us most often.

Definitions Wallet Bills · BBPS KYC Refunds Support

Definitions

Platform
The Beyound app, website, APIs and associated systems that enable digital payments.
User / You / Payer
Any individual who uses the Platform to initiate payments, top-ups or transfers.
Beneficiary
The individual or entity receiving the payment (e.g. a biller, a bank account holder, an educational institution).
Transaction
Any payment instruction initiated through the Platform — bill payment via BBPS or transfer from the Beyound Wallet.
Wallet Service
The prepaid digital wallet that lets you load money via any supported payment mode (credit card, debit card, net banking, UPI) and transfer it onward to any bank account beneficiary.
BBPS Payments
Credit-card-backed bill payments routed through the Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) for electricity, water, gas, DTH, broadband, postpaid mobile, insurance, education and other registered billers.
KYC
"Know Your Customer" — the regulatory identity-verification process required by RBI for wallet activation and higher transaction limits. Beyound runs KYC via HyperVerge.
Third-Party Service Providers
External entities engaged to facilitate payments, verification, authentication or compliance — payment gateways, banks, card networks, KYC partners.

Wallet

How do I load money into the Beyound Wallet?
Credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI — pick whichever is convenient. Funds are credited automatically after successful payin.
Can I withdraw money from the wallet to my own bank account?
Wallet funds are intended for onward transfers to third-party beneficiaries (e.g. rent, vendor payments). Self-withdrawals to the funding account are restricted under the wallet's Permitted Use clause.
What are the per-day / per-month limits?
Limits are KYC-tier driven and surfaced inside the app after wallet activation. Min-KYC has lower caps; full KYC unlocks higher caps within RBI's prepaid instrument limits.

Bills · BBPS

Which bills can I pay?
Anything available on the Bharat Bill Payment System — electricity, water, gas, DTH, broadband, postpaid mobile, insurance, education, FASTag, municipal taxes, etc.
Why do I need credit-card to pay a bill?
You don't have to use a credit card — but Bills via BBPS on Beyound is designed so any utility can be paid via credit card, even when the biller itself doesn't accept cards directly. That's the value Beyound adds.

KYC

Why is KYC needed?
RBI mandates KYC for prepaid wallets. Min KYC happens automatically on registration; full KYC (Aadhaar / DigiLocker / PAN) is required to unlock the higher-limit Wallet and unrestricted beneficiary transfers.
How long does the KYC flow take?
~2 minutes if your Aadhaar is linked to your mobile number and you have decent lighting for the live-selfie step.

Refunds

A bill payment failed — when will I get the money back?
Failed BBPS transactions auto-reverse to the source account within 5–7 working days, in line with NPCI's BBPCU rules. If a transaction shows as "successful" but the biller didn't credit, raise a grievance and we'll trace it.
How do I raise a refund or dispute?
Email support@beyound.in with the transaction ID and reason. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy for SLAs.

Support

How do I escalate a grievance?
Start at support@beyound.in. If the L1 response doesn't resolve it, the Grievance Redressal Policy lists the L2 / L3 escalation matrix with response timelines.
I think my account is being misused. What do I do?
Email support@beyound.in immediately with subject "URGENT — account compromise" — we'll lock the wallet and the BBPS profile while we investigate.
