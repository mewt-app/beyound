Beyound Checkout

Approve once.
Then pay in one tap.

Beyound Checkout is a payment layer that sits between an online brand's checkout and the bank that issued your card. Approve your card once with your bank, and every payment after that at the same brand, up to ₹25,000, takes a single tap.

What it is

A payment layer, not a wallet.

When you pick Beyound at a brand's checkout, you approve your card once with your bank. That one approval is a standing instruction — an e-mandate — and it is what lets every later payment at that same brand go through on a single tap, with no one-time password, no redirect and no bank page in the middle.

Beyound Checkout is not a wallet. There is no balance to load and no stored value to keep track of. Each payment is charged to the card, UPI handle or bank account you chose, at the moment you tap.

Operated by BeyoundExtra Tech Private Limited · Bangalore, India

  • Nothing to top up. No balance, no stored value, no separate account to fund. Money moves from the account you approved straight to the brand.
  • Your bank does the checking. The one-time approval is authenticated by your bank with a real one-time password, the way any card mandate is.
  • Capped by design. One-tap payments are limited to ₹25,000 per payment.
  • Cancel whenever you like. The mandate can be cancelled at any time, from your Beyound account or directly with your bank.

How it works for shoppers

Three steps, then one tap.

The approval happens once, at the brand where you first choose Beyound. Every payment after that at the same brand, up to ₹25,000, skips it.

1

Choose Beyound at checkout

At an online brand's checkout, pick Beyound as your payment method. The payment sheet opens right there on the brand's page.

2

Approve your card once

First time only: approve your card with a real one-time password from your bank. That approval is a bank-authenticated e-mandate. Would rather not use a card? Pay by UPI or net banking instead.

3

Pay in one tap after that

Every repeat payment at that brand, up to ₹25,000, is a single tap. No one-time password, no redirect, no bank page.

Payment methods

Pay the way you already do.

Whether it is your first payment at a brand or an alternative to the one-tap path, these are the methods the sheet supports.

Saved card New card · 3-D Secure UPI Net banking

A new card is approved with a 3-D Secure one-time password and kept as a token — the card number itself is never stored. A UPI payment is approved in your own UPI app. Net banking takes you to your bank the way it always has.

Trust and compliance

The one tap has rules behind it.

A single tap only works because the approval underneath it was done properly, once, with your bank.

A bank-authenticated e-mandate

The first approval is an e-mandate for recurring card payments, and your bank authenticates it with a real one-time password. We never stand in for that step.

Capped at ₹25,000 a payment

One-tap charges are limited to ₹25,000 per payment. Larger amounts fall outside the one-tap path.

Cancel the mandate any time

The approval is yours to end. Cancel it whenever you like and one-tap payments at that brand stop.

Tokenised cards

A saved card is held as a token, not as a card number. The number itself is never stored.

For merchants

Your checkout page. Our payment sheet.

Beyound Checkout is built to sit inside the checkout you already have, rather than replace it. The secret key stays on your server; the browser only ever holds a short-lived session.

  1. 1

    Create the order from your server

    Your backend calls Beyound with your secret API key to create the order. The key never reaches the browser.

  2. 2

    Hand the session to the browser

    That call returns a checkout session token. Pass it to your front end when you are ready to collect payment.

  3. 3

    Render the sheet inline

    The browser uses the session token to render the payment sheet on your own checkout page. On the one-tap path the shopper is never sent away to another page.
