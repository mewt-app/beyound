What Beyound Checkout is

Beyound Checkout is a hosted payment sheet — a ready-made, branded checkout screen that a brand drops onto their website or app. When a buyer is ready to pay, the Beyound sheet appears, the buyer picks how they want to pay, and the payment completes inside it.

Think of it as the "pay now" experience, delivered as a finished product. The brand does not build a payment screen, wire up card fields, or manage the bank OTP dance. Beyound owns all of that end to end.

One integration, every method

Cards, UPI, net-banking and saved "bolt" instruments — all in a single sheet the buyer already trusts.

Nothing to build

The brand renders a sheet and waits for a confirmation. Beyound handles routing, security, and the payment UI.

Higher conversion

Fewer screens, saved instruments for repeat buyers, and a fast, familiar flow lift completed-payment rates.

Loyalty built in

Beyound Cash rewards buyers at checkout — turning a one-time payment into a reason to come back.

The one-line version

Beyound Checkout lets a brand accept any payment method through a single, polished screen — while Beyound does the heavy lifting behind it.

Why it matters

Every extra step in a checkout costs conversions. Every payment method a brand can't accept costs a sale. And every brand that builds its own checkout re-solves the same hard, regulated problems — badly. Beyound Checkout removes all three.

The buyer journey

From the buyer's point of view, paying is four moments. Everything technical happens between them, unseen.

  1. Ready to pay. The buyer finishes their cart on the brand's site and taps "Pay." The Beyound sheet slides up.
  2. Pick a method. Card, UPI, net-banking, or a saved instrument from a previous purchase. Repeat buyers often see their card already waiting.
  3. Confirm securely. The buyer completes any bank verification — an OTP, an app approval — right inside the sheet. No jarring redirects to figure out.
  4. Done. The sheet shows a confirmation. The brand is notified, the order is marked paid, and the buyer is on their way.
Why it converts

Fewer taps, familiar UI, saved instruments, and no confusing bounce between sites. The buyer stays in a single, trusted flow from "Pay" to "Paid."

How the money moves

At a business level, the flow is simple. Here it is without a single line of code.

  1. Order is created. When the buyer is ready, the brand tells Beyound "here's an order for ₹X." Beyound sets up a secure, one-time checkout session for that exact amount.
  2. Buyer pays in the sheet. Beyound presents the payment options, routes the transaction to the right processing rail, and walks the buyer through any bank verification.
  3. Money is captured. The payment clears at the bank / processor. The funds are now committed to the brand.
  4. Brand is told — authoritatively. Beyound sends the brand a signed confirmation that the order is paid (or failed). This is the single source of truth the brand acts on.
  5. Funds settle. The captured money is settled to the brand on the agreed cycle, net of processing fees.
The golden rule

A brand only treats an order as paid when Beyound's signed confirmation arrives — never on the on-screen message alone. That confirmation is guaranteed to be delivered even if the buyer closes the tab right after paying. It's what keeps the two sides' books in sync.

Who does what

The partnership is a clean division of labour. Nobody re-solves the other's problems.

The brand does
  • Tells Beyound the order amount
  • Shows the Beyound sheet on its checkout page
  • Marks the order paid when Beyound confirms
  • Ships the goods / delivers the service
Beyound does
  • Presents every payment method in one sheet
  • Routes each transaction to the right rail
  • Handles all bank security & verification
  • Sends the authoritative paid / failed confirmation
  • Manages loyalty, saved instruments & settlement

For an aggregator partner, the value is that Beyound becomes the experience layer in front of your rails — you don't have to build or maintain a checkout UI to win the volume.

Beyound Cash & loyalty

Beyound Cash is a rewards balance a buyer earns and spends at checkout. It's the difference between a payment tool and a reason to come back.

  • Earned at checkout. Buyers accumulate Beyound Cash on qualifying payments — no separate loyalty programme for the brand to run.
  • Spent at checkout. It applies directly against the next purchase, right inside the sheet.
  • Stickiness for everyone. Buyers return to spend it; brands see repeat purchases; the whole network gets more valuable.
Commercial angle

Beyound Cash turns the checkout from a cost centre into a retention lever — a differentiator most raw payment rails simply don't offer.

Trust & compliance

Payments are a regulated, high-trust business. Beyound Checkout is built so partners inherit that trust rather than rebuild it.

Sensitive data stays with Beyound

Card entry, tokenization and bank verification live inside the Beyound sheet — the brand never touches raw card data.

Tamper-proof confirmations

Every paid / failed notice is cryptographically signed, so a brand can prove it genuinely came from Beyound.

Locked to approved brands

Each brand is issued its own secret credentials; the sheet only renders for approved storefronts.

Sandbox before live

Partners test the entire flow end to end in a safe sandbox before a single rupee moves in production.
